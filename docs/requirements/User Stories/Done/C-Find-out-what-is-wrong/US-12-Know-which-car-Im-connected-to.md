# US-12 Know which car I'm connected to

**Group:** C. Find out what is wrong  
**Status:** Done

**Description:** As a car owner, I want the app to read and decode my car's VIN, so that I know the data comes from the right car.

**Design:** [Connect screen](../../../../screenshots/connect-simple.png) (car details)

## Acceptance criteria

1. After connecting, the app reads the 17-character VIN.
2. The app shows the maker and model year decoded from the VIN.
3. If the car refuses to give the VIN, the app explains that some older cars don't share it.
4. The VIN can be copied with a long press.
