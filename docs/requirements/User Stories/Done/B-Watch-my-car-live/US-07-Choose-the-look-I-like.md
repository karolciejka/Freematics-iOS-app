# US-07 Choose the look I like

**Group:** B. Watch my car live  
**Status:** Done

**Description:** As a car owner, I want to switch between the Simple, Racing and Power looks, so that the app suits how I use it.

**Design:** Any screen in the [README](../../../../../README.md), side by side

## Acceptance criteria

1. A setting switches between Simple, Racing and Power, and the header switch steps through them.
2. The change applies to all five screens at once, without reconnecting.
3. The choice is kept after the app is closed.
4. All three looks pass contrast checks in light and dark mode.
