# US-01 Find and connect to my logger

**Group:** A. Get connected  
**Status:** Done

**Description:** As a car owner, I want to find my ONE+ and connect with one tap, so that I see my car's data without any cables.

**Design:** [Connect screen, Simple](../../../../screenshots/connect-simple.png) · [Racing](../../../../screenshots/connect-racing.png)

## Acceptance criteria

1. The Connect screen lists every nearby ONE+ that offers the Freematics Bluetooth service, with its name and signal strength.
2. Tapping a logger connects to it and opens the Live screen within 10 seconds when the ignition is on.
3. If no logger is found after a scan, the screen says to check that the ONE+ has power and the right firmware.
4. If Bluetooth is off or not allowed, the app says so and links to iPhone Settings.
5. The app remembers the last logger and offers it first next time.
